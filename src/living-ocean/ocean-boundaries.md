# Ocean name boundaries

Source: Natural Earth, public domain, 1:10m geography marine polygons.
Downloaded 2026-11-15 from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_geography_marine_polys.geojson
Source SHA-256: 53f865e8ffa966cdd402145c82c5cd14ee7ce974cd0eb9a3f59f03a4cfd2d66c

The companion JSON retains the seven named ocean geometries, including holes and antimeridian splits, without coordinate simplification. North/South Pacific and North/South Atlantic labels are combined into their parent ocean names. No ocean-current data is involved.

These are generalized cartographic boundaries, not legal limits. Points within 0.05 degrees of a retained polygon edge, conflicting classifications, land, uncovered marginal seas and invalid positions return Ocean region unavailable. The artificial antimeridian seam is excluded from this uncertainty buffer. Small islands and coastlines below the source resolution cannot be resolved precisely.
